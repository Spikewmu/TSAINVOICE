Show-Up & Handoff Cadence

The standard way we confirm every booked call and hand a prospect from setter to closer - so they actually show up. Same process, every prospect, every time. Replace every [bracket] before you send; tap Copy on any message to grab it.

Why this matters

A full calendar means nothing if they don't show. Humanize every touch, and own the no-show - if a call slips, you don't complain about it, you go into the thread and do something about it.

The flow

1

At booking

Setter hands off

Setter sends the video + introduces the closer, then the closer jumps into the same thread.

2

Night before

Closer confirms

End of day, the closer confirms the call - and gives up the spot if the prospect goes quiet.

3

~9am CT, day of

Setter reminds

Morning of, the setter asks for the link, nudges the video, and bumps anyone who hasn't replied.

1 · At booking

Fire these two messages back-to-back the moment the call is set - don't wait for a reply. Then the closer steps in.

SetterMessage 1 - the video
Hey [Name], it's [Setter]. Here's that short video I mentioned - watch it before your call with [Closer] tomorrow at [time]. [VSL link]
SetterMessage 2 - the handoff
Hey [Name], I want to introduce you to [Closer]. They're one of the top advisors on this team, and I've already got them up to speed on everything we talked about - so we don't lose a beat before you two meet.
CloserJumps into the same threadmake it personal from the start
Hey [Name], great to meet you - [Closer] here. I already started reviewing what [Setter] put down from your conversation, and I'll have everything customized for our call by tomorrow.

2 · End of day (night before)

The closer confirms the call. Use the direct version for flaky lists - giving up the spot creates urgency.

CloserStandard confirmation
Hey [Name], just confirming we're good for our call tomorrow before I start customizing all your material.
CloserDirect versionfor flaky lists / BizOp offers
Hey [Name], I've got to cancel your call tomorrow if I don't hear back from you - we have a handful of other people asking to use that spot. Just let me know tonight so I know whether to hold it.

3 · Morning of (~9am CT)

The setter opens with a question that gets a reply - then branches on the answer.

SetterOpenergoal: get a reply
Hey [Name], do you have the link for our call today?
If they reply
Perfect. Did you get a chance to watch the short video [Setter] sent? That way we make the most of your time on the call.
If not watched
No worries - here's the link. Do me a favor and give it a watch before the call, then shoot me a note when you've finished.
No reply in ~1 hr

Bump the opener with a simple ? - that's your job. We annoy a little because we care.

Who owns what

Setter

  • Books the call
  • Sends the video + handoff intro at booking
  • Sends the 9am opener and the bump
  • Copies the calls onto their own calendar so they're ready the moment one no-shows

Closer

  • Jumps into the thread right after the handoff
  • Owns the end-of-day confirmation
  • Extreme ownership of no-shows - if a call doesn't show, go into the thread and act, right then
  • Treats every booked call like money on the table - because it is

The rules

1

Keep it in GHL. Every message stays in the GHL thread so the team can see it and nothing gets lost. No moving prospects to WhatsApp or iMessage.

2

Send the first one yourself. Initiate manually so you keep a pulse on your prospects; only the follow-up if/thens get automated.

3

Match the tone to the offer. Casual and warm for B2C; buttoned-up and punctual for accredited or high-level B2B. Same steps, different voice.

4

After 9pm is fine. If a message needs to go out at night, send it. Don't let the clock be the reason a call doesn't show.

New-Account Kickoff SOP

When we take over an account that already has its own reps, the client's founder must formally hand authority to the TSA manager - on a call, in front of the team. Without that endorsement, incumbent reps ignore an "outside" manager. This is the standard play.

Why this matters

Existing reps did not hire us and, in their heads, do not report to us yet. When a TSA manager starts asking for SODs/EODs or booking meetings cold, incumbents deprioritize or ignore it (we lost days to Hamza/Hayyan's team ignoring Keithen and Ra-eez). A 15-minute founder-led kickoff fixes it: the person who signs their checks visibly transfers day-to-day authority to us.

When to run it

During onboarding, at the Kickoff stage - before the TSA manager runs their first meeting or asks for a single report. Never let a manager's first contact with the team be a task request; the founder's intro comes first.

Skip only when the account has no incumbent reps (a fresh build we staff ourselves) - there is nobody to win over.

Who is in the room

  • Client founder / owner - they lead it (non-negotiable; a TSA person introducing themselves never carries the same weight)
  • TSA manager(s) assigned to the account
  • Every incumbent rep (setters + closers) on the account
  • Optional: Steve or Robb on the first one for a big account
Founder endorsement script - the founder says this, out loud, on the call
Team, quick but important one. I have brought on The Sales Agency to run our sales operation, and [Manager] is now your day-to-day sales manager. That means [Manager] speaks for me on anything sales: your daily check-ins, your numbers, your call schedule, coaching, and how we run the floor. When [Manager] asks for something, treat it exactly like it came from me, because it did. They have my full authority and my full trust. I have seen their system and it is going to make you more money, so lean in. [Manager], take it from here.

Kickoff agenda (15-20 min)

  1. Founder endorsement (script above) - transfers authority
  2. Manager intro - who they are, track record, "I am here to make you more money, not add busywork"
  3. What changes day one - SOD before the day starts, EOD checkout, where to log calls (Sales HQ), the meeting cadence
  4. What is in it for them - faster leads, better scripts, commission clarity, we handle ops so they just sell
  5. The non-negotiables - reports are required, same as everyone; missed reports get flagged
  6. Q&A - answer live; capture anything open in the FAQ

After the call

  • Manager DMs each rep individually the same day - warm and specific, "great to meet you, here is your login."
  • Add every rep to Sales HQ + the account's Slack channels before the call ends if you can.
  • First SOD/EOD expected the next working day; the manager personally confirms each rep's first submission.
  • If a rep no-shows the kickoff or ignores the manager afterward, escalate to the founder once - the endorsement is what gives that teeth.

Webinar Cadence Playbook

How we turn webinar registrants into closed deals with one repeatable cadence. The one rule: never book a brand-new opt-in straight to a sales call. Everyone goes to the webinar first, or they show up cold and say "I never signed up for a call."

The 3-phase cadence

Everything is measured from the day a lead registers for a webinar.

  1. Phase 1 - days 1 to 6: get them to attend the webinar. Setter team A calls every new registrant, confirms they will attend, sends the link. No qualifying, no pitch, just get them to show up.
  2. Phase 2 - days 7 to 14: get them to the Q&A. Setters chase everyone who attended but did not book; closers chase everyone who booked but did not close.
  3. Phase 3 - day 15 and up: get them on a sales call. Setter team B books aged leads and sends a pre-call sequence in between: VSL (condensed webinar), testimonials, and authority by association (founder on stage).

Weekly rhythm

  • Webinar: every Wednesday, 7:00 PM CT (same link + time each week).
  • Q&A: the following Thursday, for the prior week's attendees who did not close. Hosted by the founder.
  • Both run every week, so there is always a webinar this week and a Q&A for last week's group.
Setter script - confirm attendance (days 1-6)
Hey [First name], it is [Setter] with [Founder]'s team. I saw you registered for the masterclass on [Date] and wanted to make sure you are set to be there live. I will text you the link now so it is handy. Anything that would stop you from making it?
Setter script - after the webinar
Hey [First name], what were your thoughts on the training? [listen] Great, let's get you a time with the team to walk through how it applies to your situation. If they did not watch: No problem at all, I will send it right over. Can you watch it before we talk tomorrow? It will make our call way more useful for you.

Tagging (this is what makes the lists work)

  • On registration: tag webinar-registrant + a week tag webinar YYYY-MM-DD (the webinar date they registered for).
  • On attendance: tag attended YYYY-MM-DD (needs Zoom Webinar to capture the attendee list; tag manually from the export until then).
  • On booking: move to the Booked pipeline stage. On close: tag won + Won stage.

The engine: cadence-timer workflow + 3 smart lists (GHL)

Date-range filters in GHL are not truly dynamic. Instead run one workflow that flips a single phase tag as the lead ages, and let the lists filter on that tag.

Workflow "Webinar - Cadence Phase Timer": on webinar-registrant add phase-webinar → wait 6 days → swap to phase-qa → wait 8 days (14 total) → swap to phase-salescall. If won is added, strip all phase-* tags and end.

  • List 1 - Phase 1 (days 1-6): tag is phase-webinar AND not attended AND not won.
  • List 2 - Phase 2 (days 7-14): tag is phase-qa AND not won (setters work attended-not-booked; closers work booked-not-closed from the Booked stage).
  • List 3 - Phase 3 (day 15+): tag is phase-salescall AND not won.

Drop each saved list link + the webinar link/date/time in the leadership Slack channel so setters open the right list without hunting.

Reps still get paid

If a lead goes to the webinar, then books, then closes, the setter and closer are paid normally. Sending a lead to the webinar first does not cost anyone their commission.
